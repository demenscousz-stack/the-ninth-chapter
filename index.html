A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Steve,

I don't really know how to write about you without making it sound like I'm trying too hard to find the perfect words. Because the truth is, I don't think there are any.

You're just... you. And somehow, that's become one of my favorite things about you.

Nine months of knowing you means nine months of collecting little things about you without even realizing it. The way you smile. The way you joke around. The little things you say. The way you can make an ordinary conversation feel like something I want to remember.

I think that's what I love about us the most. It's never really been about the big moments. It's always been the little ones.

The ones that probably wouldn't mean anything to anyone else, but somehow mean everything to me because they're ours.

And if someday we look back at this chapter and laugh at how different everything was, I hope we still remember how it felt to be here.

Right now. Nine months in. Still learning each other. Still making memories. Still choosing to stay.

I don't know what every chapter after this will look like. But I know I want to find out.

So here's to the things we've already lived, the things we're still going to laugh about, and all the little moments we haven't even met yet.

Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life.

Happy nine months, Steve.

I love you. More quietly than words can explain, but more deeply than you'll probably ever know.

— yours.
