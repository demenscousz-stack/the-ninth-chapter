A LETTER FOR YOU

Dear Steve,

Nine months sounds like a number, but when I think about it, I don't really think about numbers.

I think about your laugh. The random conversations. The little things you do without realizing. The moments where you somehow make my day better just by being there.

I know we're not perfect. We have our bad days, misunderstandings, stupid arguments, and moments when we don't really know what to say.

But somehow, after all of that, I still look at you and think, “Yeah. It's still you.”

And honestly, I hope it stays that way for a very, very long time.

I love you.
More than this little website could ever explain.

— yours, always.
